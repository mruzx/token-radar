How implied value is computed

One function scores every protocol. No name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked.

implied = round( tvl_usd × peer multiple × (1 + clamp(change_7d, −60, 120) / 100 × 0.5) × (0.6 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4) )

The peer multiple is what the market pays per dollar of TVL for tokens that already trade in the protocol’s category: the median market-cap-per-TVL of the snapshot’s live-token protocols in that category, shrunk toward the whole market’s median with a prior weight of 8, and clamped to 0.05–1.00. A category with few live tokens barely moves its multiple; a category with none falls back to the market median. TVL and seven-day growth come from the snapshot. Age is the time since the protocol was first listed, the best record of how long it has run with no token; an unlisted age uses 365 days.
